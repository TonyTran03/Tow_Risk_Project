KEY DEFINITIONS FOR THE REPORT AND PRESENTATION
Outcome: log(tows + 1). Main count forecast: max(expm1(predicted log), 0).
Snow percentages describe model-implied tows + 1, not individual towing risk.
Snow comparisons must remain within the 30 cm cap.
Conventional OLS CIs are in the main chart; dependence-aware CIs are separate.
Extra cars are conditional model scenarios, using a training-only smearing
adjustment, and depend on the stated 0 °C winter calendar baseline.
